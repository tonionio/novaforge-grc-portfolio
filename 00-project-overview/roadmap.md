# NovaForge GRC Program Roadmap

This roadmap tracks the progression of the NovaForge Manufacturing Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Program.

## Program Foundation

| Artifact | Status | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| NF-GRC-001 — GRC Program Project Charter | Complete | Authorizes the GRC program and defines scope, objectives, stakeholders, governance, risks, deliverables, and success criteria. |
| NF-GRC-002 — Company Profile | Complete | Establishes the business, technology, data, operational, third-party, and compliance context for the program. |
| NF-GRC-003 — Organizational Chart & Governance Structure | Complete | Defines reporting relationships and security/GRC responsibilities across the organization. |
| NF-GRC-004 — Asset Inventory | Next | Establishes a documented inventory of in-scope information and technology assets to support risk assessment and control selection. |

## Program Phases

The approved Program Charter defines the following development phases:

### Phase 0 — Governance & Company Foundation
Establish the organizational context and governance foundation required to support the GRC program.

**Current status:** In progress; core foundation artifacts NF-GRC-001 through NF-GRC-003 are complete. Asset Inventory is the next deliverable.

### Phase 1 — Mini Compliance Program
Develop a structured compliance program aligned with NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0.

### Phase 2 — Enterprise Risk Management
Identify, assess, prioritize, document, and track organizational cybersecurity risks.

### Phase 3 — Third-Party Risk Management
Establish a process for evaluating and documenting cybersecurity risks associated with vendors and service providers.

### Phase 4 — Incident Response Governance
Develop and test an incident response framework defining responsibilities, escalation procedures, response activities, and documentation requirements.

### Phase 5 — Technical Compliance & SOC 2 Mapping
Evaluate technical security controls and map implementations and supporting evidence to applicable SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria.

### Phase 6 — Compliance Reporting & Dashboard
Develop metrics, KPIs, KRIs, and dashboards to communicate risk, compliance status, control maturity, and remediation progress to leadership.

### Phase 7 — Internal Audit
Conduct a simulated internal audit to evaluate control effectiveness, review evidence, document findings, and develop corrective actions.

### Phase 8 — Integrated GRC Capstone
Consolidate governance, risk, compliance, vendor risk, incident response, technical control, reporting, and audit activities into a unified NovaForge Manufacturing GRC Program.

## Program Success Criteria

The Program Charter identifies the following success criteria:

- Governance documentation is complete.
- Security policies are approved.
- Risk register is populated.
- Risk assessment is completed.
- Controls are mapped to NIST CSF 2.0.
- Executive dashboard is operational.
- Vendor assessment process is documented.
- Incident response documentation is complete.

## Current Working Point

**Next deliverable: NF-GRC-004 — Asset Inventory**

The Asset Inventory will use the Company Profile's established technology, information, operational, cloud, engineering, manufacturing/OT, and third-party environments as the basis for identifying and categorizing in-scope assets.
