## Purpose

This artifact establishes the organizational and business context used throughout the NovaForge GRC Program. It serves as the reference point for later risk assessments, control selection, policy development, third-party risk analysis, and compliance work.

## Company Overview

**Company:** NovaForge Manufacturing, Inc.  
**Industry:** Industrial Robotics & Advanced Manufacturing  
**Headquarters:** Everett, Washington  
**Founded:** 2019  
**Employees:** Approximately 185  
**Operating Region:** United States and Canada  
**Business Model:** Business-to-business design, manufacture, integration, and support of industrial robotic automation systems.

NovaForge combines mechanical engineering, robotics, software, cloud-connected services, and manufacturing operations. Its environment depends on intellectual property, engineering systems, cloud services, corporate IT infrastructure, manufacturing technology, third-party suppliers, and customer information.

## Mission

To design reliable, intelligent, and secure robotic automation systems that improve the safety, efficiency, and productivity of modern manufacturing.

## Products & Services

- **ForgeArm Series** — Industrial robotic arms used for assembly, material handling, machine tending, and repetitive manufacturing tasks.
- **ForgeVision Platform** — Computer-vision and analytics platform used to monitor robotic operations, equipment status, and production performance.
- **NovaCare Services** — Installation, integration, maintenance, remote diagnostics, software updates, and technical support.

## Operating Environment

NovaForge operates across:

- Everett, Washington headquarters and manufacturing operations
- Austin, Texas engineering and customer support operations
- A distributed remote workforce

The technology environment includes:

### Cloud & SaaS
Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, AWS, GitHub, Jira, Confluence, and Salesforce.

### Corporate IT
Windows endpoints, Linux servers, corporate Wi-Fi, VPN/remote access, network firewalls, endpoint protection, and centralized identity management.

### Engineering
CAD systems, source-code repositories, development environments, robotics simulation tools, and firmware development systems.

### Manufacturing / OT
Industrial robots, PLCs, engineering workstations, manufacturing equipment, industrial network segments, and testing systems.
