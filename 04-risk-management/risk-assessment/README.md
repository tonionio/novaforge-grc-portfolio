## Purpose

This artifact identifies, evaluates, and prioritizes realistic cybersecurity risks across NovaForge's corporate IT, cloud, engineering, manufacturing/OT, business-application, third-party, and information-asset environment.

It converts the approved NF-GRC-004 Asset Inventory into decision-ready risk scenarios with accountable owners, existing controls, treatment direction, evidence expectations, and target dates.

## Assessment Results

- 20 prioritized cybersecurity risk scenarios
- 35 of 35 baseline assets mapped to at least one scenario
- 12 Critical and 8 High inherent risks
- 0 Critical, 16 High, and 4 Moderate residual risks
- 16 residual risks currently above NovaForge's stated risk appetite

## Workbook Contents

1. **Risk Register** — threat events, vulnerabilities, assets, owners, controls, scoring, treatments, actions, and evidence.
2. **Executive Summary** — management KPIs, risk distribution, priority themes, and charts.
3. **Risk Heat Map** — formula-driven residual-risk distribution across the 5×5 matrix.
4. **Methodology & Scales** — likelihood, impact, thresholds, treatment definitions, and NIST references.
5. **Asset Scope** — all 35 approved baseline assets with scenario-coverage counts.
6. **Risk-Asset Map** — normalized mapping between risk scenarios and affected assets.
7. **Revision History** — draft and approval record.

## Methodology

NovaForge uses a qualitative 5×5 model:

- **Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**
- **Low:** 1–4
- **Moderate:** 5–9
- **High:** 10–16
- **Critical:** 17–25

Inherent risk reflects exposure before considering current controls. Residual risk reflects the remaining exposure after current controls. Planned treatments do not reduce residual ratings until they are implemented and supported by evidence.

The assessment is informed by NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 and uses NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 category mappings. NovaForge's exact 5×5 thresholds are a portfolio methodology and are not prescribed by NIST.
